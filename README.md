## 📋 How working with me actually looks

No vague e-mail threads. Two documents, both downloadable as fillable PDFs
before you commit to anything:

* a **project brief** that pins down what gets built, and
* a **cooperation agreement** covering scope, deadline, two rounds of
  revisions, how scope changes get priced, and copyright passing to you on
  payment.

Payment and invoicing run through an escrow platform, so your money is held
until you accept the work. You know the price, the deadline and the terms
before I write a line of code.
